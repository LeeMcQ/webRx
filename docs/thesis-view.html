Target & heading

Bearings need a directional antenna (Yagi, loop, or your body as a shield); a whip gives an almost round plot. The phone compass reads magnetic north — add the local declination (about −25° in the Western Cape) to the offset for true bearings. Each fix is drawn as a line on the map.

What a browser can and can't see

  • Can: connection type, effective type (4g/3g…), estimated downlink and round-trip time, data saver (Network Information API, Chrome/Edge). Shown above and logged when the source is Wi-Fi or Cellular.
  • Can't: scan Wi-Fi networks, read RSSI/SSID/BSSID, or read cell ID, band or signal (RSRP/RSRQ). No browser exposes these.
  • For real RF: use an SDR in SA — tap a band below to tune it. For phone radio readings, the Radio Bridge Android app (branch radio-bridge, in progress) is planned to feed Wi-Fi, cellular, BLE and GNSS data into RM.

Band tables tap to tune in SA

RTL-SDR tunes up to about 1.7 GHz; HackRF up to 6 GHz. Bands above your receiver's range are marked.

RM · Remote Monitoring

Remote Monitoring coming next

Connect remote webRx nodes and see their measurements here, next to your own. Planned building blocks:

  • Same record format as the recorder and DT fixes, so a remote node's data lands on the MT map and DT plot unchanged.
  • Radio Bridge (branch radio-bridge, Android, work in progress): a phone app that serves Wi-Fi, cellular, BLE and GNSS readings over local HTTP.
  • A relay (WebSocket/WebRTC) for nodes outside your network — needs a hosting decision first.

Pre-drive calibration

Measure the receiver's own noise and the vehicle's noise at the settings you'll drive with, then set the threshold a guard band above the noise floor (thesis §3.4 and §4.3.1). Pins below the threshold are kept but flagged, and the map hides them by default.

1 · Check the setup

    2 · Receiver noise — 50 Ω dummy load §3.4.4.2

    1. Remove the antenna and fit the 50 Ω dummy load to the antenna port.
    2. If you can, move the receiver away from the laptop or phone on a USB extension cable.

    3 · Vehicle noise — antenna on, engine running §3.4.4.3 · §4.3.1

    1. Refit the antenna exactly as it will be for the drive (roof mount, ferrite choke, short coax).
    2. Start the engine and switch on the air-con, lights and radio.
    3. Run the laptop from its battery — not an inverter or car charger.
